Privacy, in plain language.

Source Trail Workbook is designed so your research stays yours.

What the app stores

Your workbook—including your name if you enter it, assignment question, searches, source notes, quotations, and claims—is stored in IndexedDB inside your browser on this device.

What leaves your device

No workbook content is uploaded to us. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or third-party runtime scripts. Your browser may request ordinary app files from our host.

Exports and deletion

When you export JSON, CSV, or text, your browser creates a local file. You choose where it goes and who receives it. Clear this site’s storage in your browser to delete its saved workbook. Export first if you need a copy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
