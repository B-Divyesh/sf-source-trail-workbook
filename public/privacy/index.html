Privacy, in plain language

Source Trail Workbook is designed so your research stays yours.

What the app stores

Your workbook is stored inside your browser on this device. It can include your name, assignment, searches, source notes, quotations, and claims.

What leaves your device

No workbook content is uploaded to us. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or third-party scripts.

Your browser requests ordinary app files from our host.

Exports and deletion

Your browser creates exported JSON, CSV, and text files. You choose where those files go and who receives them.

Clear this site’s browser storage to delete saved work. Export first if you need a copy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
